# HELP App TOS (Simplified)

The contents of the HELP app such as text, graphics, images, and other material is for informational and educational purposes only. The material on the HELP app is not intended to be a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.  Please consult with your health care team for personalized medical advice. Discuss any questions you may have regarding a medical condition or treatment with your health care team. Never disregard or delay seeking medical advice because of something you have read on the HELP app.
